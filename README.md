# HeartRate
This is a niche app I built as a part of my Svelte 5 practicing.

## How it works
1. Press on "Start counting".
2. Put a finger on your neck and count your heart beats until the time on the screen runs out.
3. Write how many beats you counted, and then the app will help you calculate your actual heartrate.

## An important note
This application is just a study showcase of Svelte 5. It is NOT a true replacement of a real heart rate monitor, and it should NOT be used as an indicator of your true physical health state. Use this app at your own risk, as it comes with no warranty.
